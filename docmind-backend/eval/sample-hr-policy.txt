Northwind Analytics — Employee Handbook

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY

1.1 Paid Time Off (Full-Time Employees)
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly at
a rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused PTO may be carried over into the following year up to a
maximum of 10 days. Any balance beyond 10 days is forfeited on December 31st unless local law
requires otherwise.

1.2 Leave Policy for Interns
Interns are not eligible for standard PTO. Instead, interns accrue 1 day of paid leave for every
full month of their internship, up to a maximum of 6 days for a standard 6-month internship.
Intern leave does not carry over between internship terms and does not carry over if the intern
converts to a full-time role; full-time PTO accrual begins fresh on the employee's full-time
start date.

1.3 Sick Leave
All employees, including interns, receive 8 paid sick days per year. Sick leave does not carry
over year to year. A doctor's note is required for sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive
days.

1.4 Parental Leave
Full-time employees with at least 12 months of tenure are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental
leave following the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child. Interns and employees with less
than 12 months of tenure are not eligible for paid parental leave but may request unpaid leave
of up to 8 weeks, subject to manager approval.

SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK POLICY

2.1 Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to work remotely up
to 3 days per week. Interns are not eligible for remote work and are required to work on-site
for the full duration of their internship, except in cases of documented illness.

2.2 Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home office stipend to employees
approved for regular remote work. Interns are provided a laptop only; the home office stipend
does not apply to interns.

2.3 Core Hours
All employees, remote or on-site, are expected to be available between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in
their local time zone, Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

SECTION 3: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

3.1 General Policy
Employees may submit expense reports for business-related travel, client meals, and approved
software subscriptions. All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense
being incurred, along with an itemized receipt. Reports submitted after 30 days will not be
reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances approved by a director.

3.2 Travel Expenses
Domestic flights must be booked in economy class unless the flight duration exceeds 6 hours, in
which case premium economy is permitted. Hotel accommodations are capped at $220 per night in
major metro areas and $150 per night elsewhere.

3.3 Meals
Meal reimbursement while traveling is capped at $75 per day, itemized. Alcohol is not
reimbursable under any circumstances.

SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT

4.1 Workplace Behavior
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect. Harassment,
discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in immediate
termination, regardless of the employee's tenure or role.

4.2 Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, board membership, or financial interest that
could reasonably be seen as conflicting with Northwind Analytics' business. Disclosures should
be made to the employee's manager and to HR within 2 weeks of the conflict arising.

4.3 Confidentiality
Employees, including interns, must sign a confidentiality agreement before their first day. This
agreement remains in effect for 2 years following the end of employment, covering client data,
trade secrets, and unreleased product plans.

SECTION 5: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

5.1 Review Cycle
Full-time employees receive formal performance reviews twice a year: a mid-year check-in in June
and an annual review in December. New hires receive an additional 90-day review with their
manager.

5.2 Intern Reviews
Interns receive a single formal review at the midpoint of their internship and a final review in
the last week of their term. The final review includes a recommendation on whether the intern
will be extended an offer for full-time employment.

5.3 Promotion Cycles
Promotions are considered during the December annual review cycle only. Off-cycle promotions
require director-level approval and are reserved for exceptional circumstances, such as
retention risk or a significant scope change.

SECTION 6: IT AND SECURITY POLICY

6.1 Device Security
All company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption and the company-managed antivirus
agent installed before being used to access internal systems. Personal devices may not be used
to access internal systems except through the approved mobile email client.

6.2 Password Requirements
Passwords for internal systems must be at least 14 characters, rotated every 180 days, and must
not be reused across the last 10 passwords. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all
employees and interns accessing company systems remotely.

6.3 Data Classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted data
(such as customer payment information) may only be accessed by employees who have completed the
annual Restricted Data Handling certification.

SECTION 7: COMPANY HOLIDAYS

Northwind Analytics observes 11 paid holidays per year in the United States: New Year's Day,
Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor
Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. Employees in
other countries follow the locally published holiday calendar for their office.
